Scan & Identify

Take front and back photographs. NumiPrism saves both immediately, then attempts identification and opens the appropriate collection even if recognition fails.

Take BOTH photos: identification starts automatically after both sides are captured; failures will not block saving or navigation. No typing or Save button. Photo capture automatically runs OCR and free on-device visual matching. First use downloads a large AI model and requires internet; it can take 60 seconds or more. OCR and AI errors are recorded in item notes. Results are suggestions, not authentication. The app does not yet reliably detect errors or appraise values, so uncertain items remain in My Collection.
